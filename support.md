# Flow AI Video Companion Support

For help with the Canva companion, contact [support@flowaivideo.org](mailto:support@flowaivideo.org).

Include the action you tried, the error message, browser version, and a screenshot with private information removed. Do not send passwords, API keys, payment details, or confidential videos.

## Cover cannot be loaded

A cover may open normally in a browser but still fail cross-origin loading. Choose a local PNG or JPEG cover instead. The companion resizes and uploads it to Canva, then uses the uploaded image during video import. Files must be smaller than 7 MB.

## Video cannot be imported

Use a direct public HTTPS MP4 URL, not a webpage containing a player. Ensure it works without an account, is not expired, and allows retrieval by Canva. Only use content you have permission to import.

## Import succeeds

The message **Video added to your design** means Canva accepted the asset and insertion request. Play the clip and check your design before sharing it. This message is not confirmation that your design has been publicly published.

## Creating a new video

Visit [Flow AI Video](https://flowaivideo.org/) to generate a clip outside Canva. The website may require an account or paid plan. The companion imports existing clips; it does not generate them itself.

## Availability

The companion is under development and review preparation. It is not yet publicly listed in Canva Apps.
