# Flow AI Video companion demonstration

This short screen capture shows the independent companion running from its uploaded app bundle in Canva. The app is under development and is not yet listed in the Canva Apps marketplace.

## What the recording shows

1. The empty import form.
2. A public MP4 link and a local cover image selected for import.
3. The upload and successful insertion of the video into a Canva design.
4. Playback of the inserted clip.
5. An error for an unsupported HTTP video URL.

The recording is a silent sequence of actual screen captures sampled at low frame rate. Loading times are not a performance benchmark.

## Access and limits

No external account or payment is needed to import an existing, accessible video in this companion. Creating new videos takes place separately on the [Flow AI Video website](https://flowaivideo.org/) and may require an account or paid plan. The companion does not generate videos inside Canva.

Use only content you have permission to import. Public MP4 links must work without login. A local PNG or JPEG cover can be used when a cover URL cannot be fetched. Canva stores the imported assets; this companion has no separate backend database.
