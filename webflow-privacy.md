# Flow AI Video Webflow Extension Privacy Notice

Effective date: 11 October 2026.

This notice covers the independent Flow AI Video Designer Extension for Webflow, not Webflow itself or the external [Flow AI Video website](https://flowaivideo.org/).

## Data processed

The extension reads the video and optional poster URLs that you enter and the Webflow element you select. It uses Webflow's Designer API to insert a native video element with those URLs and playback attributes. It does not request a Flow AI Video account, scan other Webflow sites, or collect analytics or advertising data.

The extension has no separate server-side storage for the URLs or your site content. Webflow stores the resulting page content under its own policies. When the site is previewed or published, visitors' browsers may request the video and poster directly from their media host, which can receive normal request information such as an IP address.

Opening the Flow AI Video website link is a separate navigation. That website may process generation, account, and payment data under its own policies. This extension does not send your Webflow page content to it.

## Your choices

Remove the inserted element in Webflow Designer to remove the media references from the page. To remove a published version, republish the site after editing it. Avoid confidential, signed, or expiring media URLs.

## Contact

For extension privacy questions, email [support@flowaivideo.org](mailto:support@flowaivideo.org). Do not send passwords, API keys, or private media in support messages.
