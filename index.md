# Flow AI Video Companion for Canva

Import an existing video into a Canva design. Create your clip on [Flow AI Video](https://flowaivideo.org/), then use the companion to add its public MP4 URL and a cover image to your design.

**Availability:** in development and testing. This companion is not yet listed in the Canva Apps marketplace. This documentation does not imply Canva or Google endorsement.

## What the companion does

- Opens the Flow AI Video website when you choose its website link.
- Imports a publicly accessible MP4 video into the current Canva design.
- Accepts a public cover-image URL or a local PNG/JPEG cover. Local covers are resized in your browser and uploaded to Canva for the import.
- Reports whether importing succeeds or fails.

It does not generate videos inside Canva, export your Canva designs, or request your Flow AI Video password. Video generation is an external website service and may require an account or paid plan. Importing an existing clip through the companion does not require a Flow AI Video login.

## Import a clip

1. Open the companion inside a Canva design.
2. Enter the public HTTPS link to your MP4 video.
3. Choose a local PNG/JPEG cover smaller than 7 MB. Use a cover with the same aspect ratio as the video. Alternatively, enter a public cover URL that permits cross-origin access.
4. Select **Add video to design** and wait for the success message.
5. Play the inserted video and check it before sharing your design.

Only use files you own or have permission to use. Do not paste confidential or private media links. A public URL must be reachable without logging in.
