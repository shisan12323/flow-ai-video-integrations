# Flow AI Video Webflow Extension Terms of Use

Effective date: 11 October 2026.

These terms apply to the independent Flow AI Video Designer Extension for Webflow. Webflow and the external [Flow AI Video website](https://flowaivideo.org/) have separate terms.

## Purpose

The extension places an existing public HTTPS MP4 video on a Webflow page. It does not generate, upload, or host media inside Webflow. The extension is currently in testing, so availability and compatibility may change.

## Your media

You are responsible for the video, poster, URLs, and permissions you provide. Use only media you own or have permission to publish. Do not expose confidential or unlawful material. The extension does not grant any rights to third-party content.

## External services

Webflow stores and publishes the page you edit. The media host delivers the video and poster to visitors. Those services may have their own terms and availability. The extension cannot guarantee playback from private, expired, unsupported, or blocked URLs. Creating a video at the external website may require an account or paid plan; the extension does not initiate a purchase.

## Review and support

Preview and test playback before publishing. A successful insertion message means only that Webflow accepted the element; it does not mean the page is published. Contact [support@flowaivideo.org](mailto:support@flowaivideo.org) for help. This extension is not sponsored or endorsed by Webflow.
